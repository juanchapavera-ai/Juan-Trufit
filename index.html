Your first 4 weeks

Week 1: Get comfortable. One set per machine is plenty.

Week 2: Try two sets of ten with good form.

Week 3: Add a few minutes of easy cardio.

Week 4: Increase resistance only when movements feel easy.

💚 A 15-minute walk is still a successful gym day. Consistency matters more than intensity. Exercise may support mental health, but it doesn't replace professional care when needed.
